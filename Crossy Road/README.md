# Crossy Road Game

Crossy Road is a replica of the original Crossy Road, a game where players move a chicken to avoid obstacles and cross as many roads as possible. My Crossy Road is much simpler than the original, but is also full screen rather than cut off on the sides.

## How to Play

Players can use WASD or the arrow keys to move the chicken around. The chicken will die if hit by a car, touches water, or is eaten up by the frame. The chicken can hop on logs or lily pads to cross rivers, and players will gain one point for each row crossed. The points will reset to zero once the chicken dies.

## AI Usage

I used Claude Sonnet 4.5 for most of the project, and only used Gemini at the end to clean up some animations and details. Most of the project was created in Kiro, and transported to VSCode afterwards to upload to Github. My main strategy was to make sure the game mechanics worked first, then add details about the aesthetics of the game.
